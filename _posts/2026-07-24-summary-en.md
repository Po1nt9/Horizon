## [GPT-5.5 and Claude Fable 5 Fail ActiveVision Benchmark](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1v4ns8l/gpt55_scores_106_on_activevision_humans_hit_961_r/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A new benchmark called ActiveVision reveals that frontier models GPT-5.5 and Claude Fable 5 score only 10.6% and 3.5% respectively, while humans achieve 96.1%. These models struggle significantly with tasks requiring iterative visual perception and self-correction via code. This highlights a critical limitation in current AI architectures: the inability to perform repeated visual observation and self-patching, which are essential for complex reasoning. It suggests that despite high scores on static benchmarks, these models lack the dynamic interaction capabilities required for real-world visual tasks. The ActiveVision benchmark consists of 17 tasks across three categories designed to force repeated visual perception rather than single static descriptions. GPT-5.5 scored zero on 11 of the 17 tasks even at its highest reasoning-effort tier, indicating a fundamental gap in iterative visual processing.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Justgototheeffinmoon · Jul 23, 19:20

**Background**: Active vision refers to computer vision systems that manipulate camera viewpoints to investigate environments and gather better information iteratively. Unlike traditional benchmarks that present a single static image, ActiveVision tests whether models can solve problems requiring multiple observations and adjustments, similar to how humans visually explore a scene.

## [Statistically-Lossless Quantization for LLMs Balances Accuracy and Speed](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1v5jd79/r_statisticallylossless_quantization_of_large/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers introduced a distribution-lossless quantization method that makes the output of quantized models practically indistinguishable from the original. This approach achieves significant model compression while maintaining inference acceleration, overcoming traditional trade-offs. This advancement allows for deploying large language models on resource-constrained devices without sacrificing performance fidelity. It bridges the gap between theoretical lossless compression and practical, accelerated inference in real-world applications. The method utilizes a stricter criterion called distribution losslessness to ensure output consistency. Unlike previous lossless techniques that did not accelerate inference, this new approach delivers both fidelity preservation and speed gains.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/Benlus · Jul 24, 18:16

**Background**: Model quantization reduces the precision of weights in neural networks to decrease size and improve speed, often at the cost of some accuracy. Traditional methods are typically lossy, meaning they sacrifice some fidelity for efficiency, while strictly lossless techniques usually fail to provide inference acceleration. This new research addresses the industry need for models that are both small/fast and highly accurate.
